# HTML Style Catalog

Use exactly one style. Do not combine palettes, component languages, or decorative motifs from multiple styles. Preserve the HTML contract regardless of style.

## 1. Studio Editorial White

- `style_id`: `studio-editorial-white`
- 中文名：Studio 编辑部白
- Best for: balanced Founder stories with problem, judgment, and Demo.
- Palette: paper `#F7F6F2`, ink `#111827`, cobalt `#2457E6`, coral `#ED4B3A`, moss `#16835F`, amber `#F4B63E`.
- Type: bold modern sans titles, restrained serif only for short quotations.
- Layout: strong editorial grid, thin rules, large photography or evidence diagram, numbered chapters.
- Signature: one sharp color rail and a full-width conclusion strip.
- Avoid: soft candy cards, gradients, ornamental illustration.

## 2. Midnight Product Launch

- `style_id`: `midnight-product-launch`
- 中文名：午夜产品发布会
- Best for: projects with a strong working Demo or striking screenshots.
- Palette: stage `#080D17`, white `#F8FAFC`, electric blue `#4D7CFE`, signal amber `#FFBF3F`, mint `#35C99A`.
- Type: large condensed-feeling sans titles, clear technical captions.
- Layout: full-bleed Demo frames, spotlighted product UI, sparse copy, cinematic chapter transitions.
- Signature: dark stage with one bright product surface.
- Avoid: glowing blobs, neon overload, tiny dashboard text.

## 3. Future Lab

- `style_id`: `future-lab`
- 中文名：未来实验室
- Best for: AI experiments, prototypes, tests, and evidence still being validated.
- Palette: lab white `#F3F5F4`, carbon `#101516`, deep teal `#0D5147`, test red `#E6493D`, safety yellow `#F2C14E`.
- Type: utilitarian sans, compact uppercase labels, monospaced evidence tags.
- Layout: experiment numbers, specimen frames, annotation lines, before/after test panels.
- Signature: every claim looks attached to an observation or test status.
- Avoid: fake scientific numbers, decorative circuit patterns, excessive grids.

## 4. Blueprint Workshop

- `style_id`: `blueprint-workshop`
- 中文名：工程蓝图工作台
- Best for: products whose key value is a workflow, system, or software architecture.
- Palette: blueprint `#123D75`, white `#F8FBFF`, cyan `#46B5D1`, orange `#F28C28`, charcoal `#18202B`.
- Type: sturdy geometric sans with monospaced step labels.
- Layout: process maps, dimension lines, numbered modules, one large system drawing per page.
- Signature: “problem to product” is shown as a buildable mechanism.
- Avoid: unreadable engineering detail, fake code, dark blue covering every surface.

## 5. Founder Newsroom

- `style_id`: `founder-newsroom`
- 中文名：Founder 头版新闻
- Best for: human stories, a clear social observation, or projects with useful documentary photos.
- Palette: newsprint `#F5F1E8`, black `#111111`, press red `#C8322E`, royal blue `#2554A6`, olive `#67734A`.
- Type: editorial serif headlines paired with clean sans body text.
- Layout: headline, deck, captioned photo, pull quote, evidence column.
- Signature: the problem feels newsworthy and specific.
- Avoid: long newspaper paragraphs, nostalgia filters, decorative fake articles.

## 6. Mission Control

- `style_id`: `mission-control`
- 中文名：任务控制中心
- Best for: products with a clear sequence, checkpoints, states, or a live operating flow.
- Palette: graphite `#121820`, panel white `#F4F7F9`, command green `#4FC08D`, alert orange `#FF8A34`, active blue `#4B73FF`.
- Type: direct interface sans with stable numeric counters.
- Layout: mission stages, state indicators, command path, progress rail, large central outcome.
- Signature: every page answers “Where are we, what happens next, who decides?”
- Avoid: sci-fi decoration, dense dashboards, blinking animation.

## 7. Product Gallery

- `style_id`: `product-gallery`
- 中文名：产品展览馆
- Best for: visually strong physical-digital products, illustrations, interfaces, or brand concepts.
- Palette: gallery white `#FAFAF8`, ink `#151515`, ultramarine `#2448D8`, vermilion `#E54836`, leaf `#2F7D57`.
- Type: elegant oversized sans with museum-label captions.
- Layout: one hero object per page, generous border space, narrow evidence plaque, deliberate scale.
- Signature: the product is treated as the object worth inspecting.
- Avoid: many small cards, stock-like atmosphere, hiding the real product.

## 8. Investor Data Room

- `style_id`: `investor-data-room`
- 中文名：投资人数据室
- Best for: projects with credible sizing, tests, usage counts, comparisons, or measurable outcomes.
- Palette: white `#FFFFFF`, navy `#17233C`, data blue `#2F64D6`, positive green `#16845B`, caution amber `#D99614`, exception red `#C6473A`.
- Type: highly legible sans, tabular numerals, compact evidence labels.
- Layout: one decision per page, one primary chart, assumptions table, source footer.
- Signature: every number shows its status and implication.
- Avoid: invented metrics, chart walls, miniature labels, finance-corporate clichés.

## 9. Field Notes

- `style_id`: `field-notes`
- 中文名：真实观察手记
- Best for: early-stage ideas grounded in observations, interviews, sketches, and iterative discoveries.
- Palette: clean paper `#FBFAF6`, ink blue `#1C3557`, highlighter yellow `#F3C94B`, observation red `#D94B3D`, field green `#4C7A61`.
- Type: editorial sans with a restrained handwritten accent used only for annotations.
- Layout: observation card, annotated photo, evidence pin, crossed-out assumption, revised conclusion.
- Signature: show what the Founder noticed, changed, and still needs to test.
- Avoid: childish scrapbook decoration, fake handwriting everywhere, low contrast beige pages.

## 10. Cinematic Founder Story

- `style_id`: `cinematic-founder-story`
- 中文名：电影式 Founder 故事
- Best for: projects with a vivid real-world moment and a simple emotional transformation.
- Palette: black `#0D0F12`, warm white `#F5F2EA`, cobalt `#315AEF`, coral `#EB5142`, warm gold `#D8A53A`.
- Type: large cinematic sans titles with small precise captions.
- Layout: full-bleed scene, short statement overlays, storyboard sequence, hard cuts between chapters.
- Signature: begin with a moment, reveal the system, end with a concrete next scene.
- Avoid: atmospheric images that hide the product, dark unreadable screenshots, movie-poster decoration.

## Selection Display

When asking the user to choose, show:

1. Studio 编辑部白：均衡、克制，适合大多数项目。
2. 午夜产品发布会：深色舞台，最突出 Demo。
3. 未来实验室：适合 AI 实验、测试和待验证证据。
4. 工程蓝图工作台：适合流程、系统和构建逻辑。
5. Founder 头版新闻：适合真实人物故事与社会观察。
6. 任务控制中心：适合步骤、状态和工作流产品。
7. 产品展览馆：适合视觉成果和产品本身很强的项目。
8. 投资人数据室：适合有可靠数字、比较和测试的项目。
9. 真实观察手记：适合从观察、访谈和迭代出发的项目。
10. 电影式 Founder 故事：适合场景鲜明、转变清楚的产品故事。
